VeyCut 0.2.0 caption sample

The editor includes an English interface. Choose English in Settings > Language when needed.

Use bilingual.vtt OR bilingual.srt, not both in the same timeline.

1. Create a portrait 720 x 1280 project at 30 fps.
2. Open Captions, then Files. Import one sample at timeline start.
3. In Edit, choose all text clips and apply bottom placement, medium size and outlined appearance.
4. Shift the text clips by 0.5 seconds, then Undo to restore their timing.
5. Replace the exact text "VeyCut" with "My story", then Undo. Replacement is case-sensitive.
6. Export the text clips as SRT or WebVTT and inspect their text and timing.
7. Save the project and reopen it.

Subtitle files carry text and timing; font, placement and appearance are project settings. These samples are prepared for manual checking. The packaged-app manual flow and clean-machine installation have not yet been verified.
